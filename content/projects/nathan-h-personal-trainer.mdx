## **Project Overview:**

A conversion-focused marketing site for Nathan Henrique Dias, an online personal trainer (CREF-certified, USP-educated) coaching clients directly since 2018. The site walks visitors through his coaching process, before/after client results, testimonials, and subscription plans, closing with a clear path to sign up.

## **Tech Stack:**

- **Next.js:**
  Fast, SEO-friendly delivery for a page whose whole job is converting visitors into clients.
- **Tailwind CSS:**
  Styling for the bold, dark editorial look built around his training photography.
- **Vercel:**
  Deployment and hosting.
