## **Project Overview:**

Silicon Data runs a data intelligence platform that gives financial institutions, compute buyers, and data centers systematic visibility into the GPU and AI compute economy — aggregating 3.5M+ data points into daily pricing benchmarks (Silicon Data Indices), performance benchmarking (SiliconMark™), predictive pricing (SiliconPriceIQ™), and capacity analysis (SiliconSiteIQ™), distributed through channels like Bloomberg and Refinitiv.

I'm a Full Stack Software Engineer there, working on SiliconMark — the backend and benchmarking tooling that validates GPU throughput and latency and feeds the pricing indices institutional clients rely on.

## **Tech Stack:**

- **SiliconMark backend:**
  Backend development for GPU performance benchmarking — the layer that validates throughput and latency data feeding the Indices.
- **Data pipeline reliability:**
  Working within a systems where correctness matters — the benchmarks underwrite institutional pricing decisions.
